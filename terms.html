All policies

Lovecargameplay Uploader is a private publishing tool used only by the operator of the Lovecargameplay channel. It uploads the channel's own original physics-simulation car videos to the channel's own TikTok account. It is not offered to the public, and no other TikTok users can sign in to it.

Terms of Service

Who may use it

Only the Lovecargameplay channel operator uses this tool, with the operator's own TikTok account. Nobody else is granted access.

What it does

  • Signs in to TikTok with Login Kit, after the account owner approves it on TikTok's own consent screen.
  • Uploads videos and their captions with the Content Posting API.
  • Reads the account's basic profile to confirm it is posting to the right account.

Content

Every uploaded video is created by Lovecargameplay: our own physics simulation, original vehicle designs and original music. The tool never uploads other people's content. All posts follow TikTok's Community Guidelines and Terms of Service.
