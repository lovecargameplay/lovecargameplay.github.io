Lovecargameplay Uploader Privacy Policy

Effective 1 November 2026

All policies

Lovecargameplay Uploader is a private publishing tool used only by the operator of the Lovecargameplay channel. It uploads the channel's own original physics-simulation car videos to the channel's own TikTok account. It is not offered to the public, and no other TikTok users can sign in to it.

Privacy Policy

Data the tool receives from TikTok

  • An access token and a refresh token, so the tool can upload to the approved account.
  • Basic profile information (open ID, display name and avatar), used only to check which account is connected.
  • The publish status of each upload.

How it is stored

Tokens are kept in a private file on the operator's own computer, readable only by the operator. They are not stored on any server and are not shared with anyone.

What we never do

  • We do not collect data about other TikTok users or viewers.
  • We do not sell, rent or share any data.
  • We do not use the data for advertising or profiling.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy, Security, Apps and services permissions. Removing access stops all uploads. The operator then deletes the stored tokens.

Contact

Questions can be sent through the Lovecargameplay YouTube channel: youtube.com/@lovecargameplay.
